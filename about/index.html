About

Fernando Enterprise is Antonio Esquivel, an independent developer in California building regulation and compliance reference apps for the state. The apps are unofficial: not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by any agency.

The rule that shapes every one of them is the same. Every value shows the official source it came from, its citation, and the date it was checked — and when something is not published yet, the app says so instead of guessing.
