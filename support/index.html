Help with CA Hunt & Fish Guide

The app keeps its own support page and issue tracker. Use those first — they are where app problems get answered and tracked.

  • CA Hunt & Fish Guide support Common questions, subscription help, and how to get in touch about the app.
  • Issue tracker Report a bug, or a regulation you believe the app has wrong. Include the screen you were on.

If a rule in the app looks wrong, say which rule and where you saw the correct version. Every value in the app carries a citation and a check date, so a report that names them can be settled quickly.
