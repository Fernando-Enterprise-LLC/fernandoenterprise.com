Independent regulation apps for California

For people who would rather check than guess.

Fernando Enterprise builds independent, unofficial regulation and compliance reference apps for California. The rules are complicated, they change, and the consequence of getting one wrong falls on the person who got it wrong. These apps exist to make the actual rule, and where it came from, easy to find.

Fernando Enterprise is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by any agency.

Every rule shows its source

Every value in these apps shows the official source it came from, its citation, and the date it was checked. When something is not published yet, the app says so instead of guessing.

That is the whole product principle, and it is the reason to trust one of these apps over a memory or a forum post: you can always go and read the rule yourself.
